How this works

Updated automatically, every week

A scheduled job runs every Monday. It refreshes the official death counts from the CDC and San Francisco open-data APIs, then searches for new reports, checks each one against its source, and adds those that qualify. Reports added in the past week carry a yellow ring on the map.

Trusted primary sources only

Every report links to the organization that produced the information: health departments, medical examiners, the CDC and DEA, the Federal Register, attorneys general, police and courts, and drug-checking programs. News coverage alone does not qualify.

Points, not a heat map

A few dozen reports of different kinds would make a heat map of where news happens, not where people overdose. Each point is one report, placed approximately. For how many people are dying, use the official counts above.

Data and corrections

The data files are public: incidents.json and trend.json. Official counts come from CDC state counts, CDC county counts and the San Francisco Medical Examiner. Spotted a mistake? Open an issue.
